Foundations (13 elements)

  • F.01 Practice Mission Statement

    Document · Core · Audit

    Make responsible AI adoption practical, measurable, and sustainable for organizations running Salesforce, grounded in clean data, human oversight, and documented governance.

    Control: Anchor every engagement decision back to this. Drift here is drift everywhere.

  • F.02 Engagement Scope (In)

    Document · Core

    Defined services CCC delivers: governance frameworks, Agentforce/Einstein/Data Cloud readiness, data quality audits, human-in-the-loop protocols, regulatory mapping, training, AI Decision Log setup, agentic governance.

    Control: Scope creep starts when a client asks for something here that lives next door. Note it, scope it, repackage it.

  • F.03 Engagement Scope (Out)

    Document · Core

    Explicitly excluded: custom AI/ML model development, data science services, non-Salesforce AI platforms, legal advice on AI regulations.

    Control: State out-of-scope items in writing at engagement kickoff. Disagreements about scope happen six weeks in, not on day one.

  • F.04 Principle: Data First

    Document · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    No AI feature is activated until data quality is assessed. Clean data is a prerequisite, not a parallel workstream.

    Control: This is the principle that gates Checkpoint 1. If data quality scores below 7, engagement does not proceed to AI activation.

  • F.05 Principle: Human Oversight

    Document · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Every AI-assisted decision has a documented human review gate. Automated actions are bounded by explicit scope limits.

    Control: For agentic engagements: every data-modifying action requires a human confirmation gate. Read-only can proceed autonomously.

  • F.06 Principle: Transparency

    Document · Core · Audit · AI surface

    AI governance documentation written in plain language for four audiences: executives, project managers, technical teams, end users.

    Control: Test by reading aloud to a non-technical stakeholder. If it needs a translator, it's not transparent yet.

  • F.07 Principle: Accountability

    Document · Critical · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Every AI deployment has a named human owner responsible for outcomes. AI does not make consequential decisions without human authorization.

    Control: Named owner in writing, in the engagement intake. No 'TBD' allowed at deployment.

  • F.08 Principle: Proportionality

    Document · Core · Mutation · AI surface

    Governance controls are proportional to risk. Low-risk automations receive lighter review. High-impact AI decisions receive mandatory multi-step validation.

    Control: Use case risk classification feeds checkpoint depth. Don't apply enterprise governance to a Lead routing automation.

  • F.09 Principle: Reversibility

    Document · Critical · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Every AI deployment includes a documented rollback procedure. If something goes wrong, the system returns to its pre-AI state within a defined timeframe.

    Control: Test the rollback before launch. An untested rollback is not a rollback.

  • F.10 NIST AI RMF 1.0

    Document · Core · Audit · AI surface

    Primary organizing framework. Four functions (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage) structure all governance checkpoints. Reference includes NIST AI RMF 1.0 (Jan 2023), the Generative AI Profile NIST-AI-600-1 (originally July 2024, updated April 8, 2026), and the Critical Infrastructure Profile concept note (April 7, 2026).

    Control: Every checkpoint maps to a NIST function. If you can't map it, you don't need it.

  • F.11 ISO 42001 Controls

    Document · Supporting · Audit · AI surface

    Controls reference for rigor. Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle informs continuous improvement. Future certification target.

    Control: Procurement teams at enterprise prospects ask for ISO 42001 alignment. Track gaps quarterly.

  • F.12 Salesforce Trusted AI

    Document · Core · PII · Audit · AI surface

    Platform-level governance. Einstein Trust Layer, audit trails, topic classification, data masking. HIPAA Security Rule overhaul currently on the OCR regulatory agenda for May 2026 finalization, though delays or modifications are possible. If finalized, expect prescriptive cybersecurity requirements for ePHI.

    Control: Verify Trust Layer masking is active for all sensitive fields before any agent goes live.

  • F.13 EU AI Act

    Document · Core · PII · Audit · AI surface

    August 2, 2026 enforcement date for Annex III high-risk systems remains legally in force. The Digital Omnibus deferral package failed at the April 28, 2026 trilogue; a follow-up trilogue is scheduled for around May 13, 2026. The dispute concerned Annex I conformity assessment for embedded AI in regulated products, not Annex III. Plan against the binding August 2 deadline; a delay is possible but not guaranteed.

    Control: Classify every use case under EU AI Act risk tiers. High-risk systems trigger additional governance.

Checkpoints (6 elements)

  • C.01 CP1: Data Readiness

    Process · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: Data quality score 7/10 or higher. Duplicates below threshold. Sharing model audited. NIST function: MAP. Documentation: Data Quality Assessment Report.

    Control: This gate fails most often. Build the audit findings into the engagement plan from day one.

  • C.02 CP2: Permission and Access

    Process · Critical · PII · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: Field-level security verified. AI user access matrix documented. Einstein Trust Layer configured. NIST function: GOVERN. Documentation: Permission Audit Log.

    Control: Trust Layer must be on. Verify with a screenshot of the configuration, not a verbal confirmation.

  • C.03 CP3: Design Review

    Process · Critical · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: Use case risk classified. Human oversight requirements defined. Escalation protocols documented. NIST functions: MAP + GOVERN. Documentation: AI Decision Log Entry.

    Control: Risk classification anchors checkpoint depth downstream. Get this wrong and every later step is sized incorrectly.

  • C.04 CP4: Agentic Governance Rev

    Process · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: Agent authorization boundaries defined. Agent Fabric guardrails configured. Agent Script business logic validated. Session Tracing enabled. Human confirmation gates set for data-modifying actions. NIST functions: GOVERN + MAP. Documentation: Agentic Governance Checklist.

    Control: Added v1.1 for Headless 360. Skip this for agentic engagements and you've shipped an unguarded agent.

  • C.05 CP5: Pre-Production Val

    Process · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: Test results documented. Bias checks completed. Rollback procedure tested. Audit logging confirmed. Agent-triggered workflow validation completed. NIST function: MEASURE. Documentation: Validation Report.

    Control: Untested rollback is not a rollback. The test must produce evidence.

  • C.06 CP6: Post-Launch Gov

    Process · Core · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Gate criteria: 30-day monitoring review. Drift indicators baselined. Governance handoff documentation complete. Agent behavior audit completed. NIST function: MANAGE. Documentation: Post-Launch Governance Report.

    Control: Most failures show up between day 7 and day 30. The 30-day review catches them before they become incidents.

Tools and Frameworks (6 elements)

  • T.01 AI Readiness Scorecard

    Tool · Core · Audit · AI surface

    15-question weighted assessment across five categories: Data Quality, Governance Readiness, Automation Maturity, AI Preparedness, Documentation Health. Numerical baseline 0-100. Live at clearconciseconsulting.com/scorecard.

    Control: Scorecard results auto-route to a triage call. Track conversion rates monthly.

  • T.02 Human Validation Framework

    Tool · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Structured protocol establishing mandatory human review checkpoints at each stage of AI-assisted decision-making. Documents reviewers, conditions, escalation paths, override records, and human-AI agreement rates over time.

    Control: This is the load-bearing tool for human oversight. If HVF says no, the action does not proceed.

  • T.03 AI Decision Log

    Tool · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Salesforce-native custom object structure recording every AI-assisted decision with confidence scoring, data source verification, human review status, and override documentation. Required for OMB M-25-21 compliance. M-25-21 implementation deadline elapsed April 2026; federal AI use cases are now in active enforcement, not preparatory phase. Companion memo M-25-22 governs federal AI procurement (applies to solicitations after October 1, 2025).

    Control: Required for OMB M-25-21 compliance. Set retention period in writing per regulatory baseline.

  • T.04 Agentic Governance Checklist

    Tool · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    14-item pre-deployment checklist for engagements involving Agentforce, Headless 360 MCP tools, or third-party agents. Authorization boundaries, integration user permissions, Agent Fabric, Agent Script, human gates, Session Tracing, validation rules, governor limits, rollback plan, agent code review.

    Control: All 14 items must be met before any agent goes live. No partial credit.

  • T.05 10-Metric Data Quality Audit

    Tool · Critical · PII · Audit

    SOQL-based 10-metric data quality assessment. Completeness, accuracy, consistency, validity, uniqueness, timeliness, integrity, conformity, lineage, accessibility. Output: Data Quality Score 0-10.

    Control: Score below 7 fails Checkpoint 1. Document remediation plan as Phase 1 of any engagement.

  • T.06 Agent Authorization Matrix

    Tool · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Documented mapping of each agent to its integration user permissions, MCP tool access, action boundaries, and approval gates. Required for every agentic engagement.

    Control: Without this matrix, an agent's authorization is implicit. Implicit authorization is invisible authorization.

Operating Model (8 elements)

  • O.01 CoE Director

    Process · Critical

    Practice strategy, framework development, quality standards, regulatory monitoring, thought leadership, advisory network management. Single role holds strategy, delivery, QA, and thought leadership authority.

    Control: Role consolidation eliminates coordination overhead but creates capacity ceiling. Track utilization to know when to delegate.

  • O.02 AI Governance Advisor

    Process · Core · PII · Audit · AI surface

    Engagement role. Risk assessments, governance framework design, compliance mapping, human oversight protocol development, checkpoint enforcement.

    Control: This role enforces the gates. Authority to halt an engagement is non-negotiable.

  • O.03 Data Readiness Lead

    Process · Core · PII · Mutation · Audit

    Engagement role. Data quality audits, SOQL-based 10-metric assessment, deduplication analysis, AI data preparation, field-level security review.

    Control: Data Readiness Lead's report drives Checkpoint 1. The 7/10 score is their authority.

  • O.04 Training Lead

    Process · Supporting · AI surface

    Engagement role. Client AI literacy workshops, Salesforce admin AI enablement, governance documentation training, EU AI Act literacy compliance.

    Control: EU AI Act Article 4 requires AI literacy training for staff using high-risk systems. This role satisfies that obligation.

  • O.05 External Advisors

    Process · Supporting

    Cultivated network of 2-3 vetted specialists. Peer review of frameworks, sector-specific compliance guidance (healthcare, government), award nomination support, overflow capacity.

    Control: Advisors are not subcontractors. Boundaries on engagement involvement protect client confidentiality and CCC IP.

  • O.06 Weekly Operations Review

    Process · Supporting · Audit

    60-minute weekly review: pipeline, active engagements, framework updates, content calendar.

    Control: Cadence drift here means the practice is reactive instead of operated. Skip two weeks and the symptoms appear in client engagements.

  • O.07 Monthly Framework Audit

    Process · Core · Audit · AI surface

    90-minute monthly framework audit: review governance tools against new regulations, Salesforce releases, client feedback.

    Control: Salesforce ships AI features in every seasonal release. Monthly cadence is the minimum to stay current.

  • O.08 Quarterly Performance Review

    Process · Core · Audit

    2-hour quarterly review: KPI dashboard, client outcomes, thought leadership metrics, award pipeline.

    Control: Quarter-over-quarter delta on leading indicators is the canary. Watch the trend, not the snapshot.

Risk Controls (7 elements)

  • R.01 Emergency Rollback

    Control · Critical · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Documented procedure to deactivate Agentforce agents, remove AI permissions, restore prior state. Trigger: AI producing harmful, inaccurate, or non-compliant outputs.

    Control: Test annually. Untested rollback is not a control.

  • R.02 Human Override Authority

    Control · Critical · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Named individual at client org empowered to halt any AI process. Trigger: any checkpoint failure or escalated concern.

    Control: Named in writing at engagement intake. Authority does not require approval to invoke.

  • R.03 Data Masking Verification

    Control · Critical · PII · Audit · AI surface

    Pre-deployment audit confirming Einstein Trust Layer masking is active for all sensitive fields. Trigger: every engagement involving AI plus PII or PHI.

    Control: Verify with screenshot, not verbal. Required artifact in Permission Audit Log.

  • R.04 Scope Boundary Enforcement

    Control · Core · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    AI agents limited to approved topics and actions via Agentforce topic classification. Trigger: Agent Builder configuration.

    Control: Topic classification is the simplest scope control. Add Agent Script for harder boundaries.

  • R.05 Quarterly Governance Review

    Control · Core · PII · Audit · AI surface

    Scheduled review of all active AI deployments against current regulatory requirements. Calendar-driven. Accelerated if regulations change.

    Control: Skipping a quarter creates compliance debt. Compliance debt compounds.

  • R.06 Agent Kill Switch

    Control · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    Documented procedure to revoke integration user access, disable MCP tools, and halt Agent Fabric orchestration for specific agents. Trigger: agent producing bulk erroneous data, exceeding authorization boundaries, or failing Session Tracing audit.

    Control: Agent acts at machine speed. Kill switch must be invokable in under five minutes by named owner.

  • R.07 Agent Data Isolation

    Control · Critical · PII · Mutation · Audit · AI surface

    All agent-created records tagged with source identifier for bulk query and reversal. Required for every agentic engagement.

    Control: Source identifier field on every object the agent can write. Without it, agent-created data is indistinguishable from human-entered data.

Metrics (7 elements)

  • M.01 Scorecards Completed

    Metric · Supporting · AI surface

    Leading indicator. Year 1 target: 100+. Source: website analytics. Top of pipeline.

    Control: Drop in scorecard volume signals content and SEO health, not just AI demand.

  • M.02 Engagement Pipeline

    Metric · Core · Audit

    Leading indicator. Year 1 target: $150K+. Source: CRM tracking.

    Control: Pipeline weighted by stage. Coverage ratio of 3x quota minimum.

  • M.03 Engagements Completed

    Metric · Core · Audit

    Lagging indicator. Year 1 target: 8-12. Source: engagement log.

    Control: Track at completion not signature. Completion is when the post-launch governance report ships.

  • M.04 CoE Service Revenue

    Metric · Core · Audit

    Lagging indicator. Year 1 target: $120K-$200K. Source: financial records.

    Control: Mix matters. Recurring (retainer) vs project. Track ratio quarterly.

  • M.05 Client Data Quality Lift

    Metric · Core · PII · Audit

    Lagging indicator. Year 1 target: 30%+ average improvement. Source: pre/post assessment comparison.

    Control: This is the outcome metric for the Data First principle. Without lift, the principle is ceremonial.

  • M.06 AI Deployment Success Rate

    Metric · Critical · Audit · AI surface

    Lagging indicator. Year 1 target: 90%+ (no failures in 90 days). Source: client follow-up.

    Control: This is the practice's reputation metric. Single failure can offset months of pipeline.

  • M.07 Prevented AI Failures

    Metric · Core · Audit · AI surface

    Lagging indicator. Year 1 target: 3+ documented with dollar values. Source: client documentation.

    Control: Case study fuel. Each prevented failure with dollar amount becomes content, sales evidence, and award submission material.
