How to read this stack map ▾

Three bands, one practice.

The top band is the Domain Map: every element of the AI CoE practice grouped into seven color-coded domains. Cell size and risk-tier color edge tell you what carries weight. Click any cell to open the detail panel. The middle band is the Governance Strip: a single horizontal view of all 55 elements scored on three governance dimensions. The bottom band is the index, legend, and orientation.

Three governance marks tell the story.

PII (red): elements that touch personal or sensitive client data. Mutation (purple): elements that involve changes to client systems, data, or configurations. Audit (blue): elements that produce or require audit evidence. The mark you do not see in the strip is as informative as the one you do. A row that lights up red is the PII surface of the entire practice. The gold dot in the corner of cells marks elements activated for AI and agentic engagements.

Three lenses, one chart.

The lens toggle at the top rotates the entire map without changing the underlying data. By Domain is the default and shows the practice as designed. By Risk emphasizes critical elements and dims supporting ones. AI Surface shows only what activates when an engagement involves Agentforce or Headless 360. Use the search to find anything by name, code, or description.
